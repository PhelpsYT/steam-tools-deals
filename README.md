# Steam Tools & Deals

A mobile app that complements Steam.

## What you can do

- **Connect your Steam account.** Sign in with Steam right inside the app.
- **See everything you own.** Your full Steam library — base games, DLC, tools, software — all in one place.
- **Browse your wishlist.** Every game you've wishlisted, with current prices.
- **Stay up to date.** The numbers refresh on their own as you move around the app, so what you see is always live.

## Why it's safe

Everything happens on your phone. There's no server, no database, no account on our side — your sign-in goes straight from your phone to Steam and back. Your password is typed into Steam's own login page (the same one you'd use in a browser), so your OS password manager works and we never see it. When you sign out, the session is closed on Steam's side too.

No third party gets your data. It's just you and Steam, with the app in the middle showing you what you already have.
